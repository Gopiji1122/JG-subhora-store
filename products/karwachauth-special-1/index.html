Description

Karwachauth Special 1 is a red, fully customizable puja set designed around a personalized couple-photo thali. The standard six-piece set contains one thali, two katoris, one diya, one chhalni and one small kalash/lota. The full set weighs approximately 1.5 kg.

The standard set price is ₹1,800 before shipping. Colour, design, photo and item selection can be customized to your needs, and the final price may vary according to the requested changes. Confirm the proposed design and total price on WhatsApp before ordering.

Material and care

The set uses stainless-steel pieces with a red decorative finish by default. The thali photo area and stone work are protected with resin. Wipe decorated surfaces gently with a soft dry or slightly damp cloth. Avoid soaking, abrasive scrubbing or rough handling of decorated areas.

What's included?
  • One decorated thali with a personalized photo centre
  • Two katoris
  • One diya
  • One chhalni
  • One small kalash/lota

The standard set contains six pieces. You can request different items, but the final selection and price must be confirmed before ordering.
