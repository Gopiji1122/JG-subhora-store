Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to burn dhoop cones or loban inside a wooden box?

Yes! The center features a heat-resistant metal cup that holds the burning dhoop or sambrani cup, keeping embers isolated from the wooden walls.

How does the smoke come out when the lid is closed?

The pyramid roof has diamond-shaped ventilation holes on the sides that allow fragrant smoke to flow upward smoothly while containing ash inside.

What types of incense can I use in this dhoop daan?

You can use standard dhoop cones, cow dung sambrani cups, camphor (kapoor), or small loban pieces inside the center metal receptacle.

How do I clean the ash after puja?

Let the metal cup cool completely after the dhoop finishes burning, open the front latch, empty the ash, and wipe the cup gently with a dry cloth.
