Pooja Thali Design and Decoration

In Hindu worship, a pooja thali (or aarti thali) is the sacred platter that arranges all offerings systematically according to Vedic customs. The 12-inch decorative stainless steel pooja thali provides ideal balance: heavy enough to stay stable during aarti movements, and spacious enough to hold unbroken akshat (chawal), kumkum, chandan, gangajal lota, and ghee diya without crowding.
