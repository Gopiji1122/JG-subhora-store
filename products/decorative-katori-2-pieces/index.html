Material and care

The base material is stainless steel. Wipe gently with a soft, slightly damp cloth and dry after cleaning. Avoid abrasive scrubbers, harsh chemicals and soaking the decorated surface, as these may loosen or damage the decorative stones and finish. Handle carefully and store separately to reduce scratches.
