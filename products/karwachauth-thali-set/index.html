Karwachauth Thali Set and Accessories

A complete Karwa Chauth thali set with lota and chalni is central to the sacred Vrat rituals observed by married women across India. During the evening puja, the decorated aarti thali holds the essential puja samagri including roli, unbroken akshat (chawal), sweets, and the lit ghee diya. When the moon rises, the decorated chhalni is used to view the moon and husband, followed by the water offering (Arghya) using the matching pooja lota.
