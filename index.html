Frequently asked questions

Helpful details before you place an order.

What material are the products made from?

Our product bases are stainless steel. Decorative stones, pearls, resin and other finishing materials may vary by design. Please check the individual product page for specific details.

Are the products water-resistant?

The stainless-steel base is water-resistant, but decorative elements and adhesives may need gentle care. Avoid soaking or scrubbing decorated surfaces; wipe gently with a soft, dry cloth unless the product instructions say otherwise.

How do I place an order?

Tap “Order Now” on a product to open WhatsApp with the product details. We’ll help confirm the design, price, availability and shipping before the order is finalised.

Can I request a custom-made design?

Yes, you can ask us about available custom colours, patterns or personalisation. Share your preferred design on WhatsApp so we can confirm feasibility and pricing.

How do shipping and returns work?

Shipping charges, delivery information and applicable return terms are explained on our Shipping & Returns page. Please review the policy before ordering.
