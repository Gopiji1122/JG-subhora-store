Karwa Chauth Thali Items List: Lota, Chhalni and More

Getting the Karwa Chauth thali ready is easier when you know exactly what goes on it. This guide gives a clear items list, ideas for decorating the karwa lota and chhalni, and a simple way to be fully prepared the day before the festival.

  • ⏱ 3 min read
  • 📅 Updated 23 Oct 2026
  • ✍ JG Subhora Store
Quick answerMost thalis include a diya, a karwa or lota, a chhalni, katoris for roli, rice and sweets, and basic puja items. The exact list varies by family and region, so confirm yours with your elders.

Karwa Chauth thali items checklist

  • A thali, preferably a clean and decorated one
  • A diya and a way to light it safely
  • A karwa or decorated lota with water
  • A chhalni (sieve) for the moon-viewing ritual
  • Small katoris for roli, rice and sweets
  • Flowers and any other puja items your family uses

Treat this as a starting point, since customs differ between families and regions.

Karwa and lota decoration ideas

  • Red mirror work: Mirrors and stones around the body make the lota look festive.
  • Pearl drops: Hanging or looped pearl lines add elegance.
  • Matching lid or cap: A lid in the same finish makes the lota look complete.
  • Clean inside: Keep the inside of the lota clean and dry before filling it.

Chhalni decoration

The chhalni is used during the moon-viewing ritual, so it is often decorated to match the rest of the thali. Bead or pearl trim around the rim looks neat. Check that the mesh is clean and the decoration does not catch on your hands or clothes.

Katoris and diya

Katoris hold roli, rice and sweets, so choose ones that are easy to hold and look good with the thali. A small decorated diya adds to the look, but always keep the flame stable and away from cloth and decoration.

Be ready the day before

  1. Check that every item on your list is present and clean.
  2. Wipe the thali, lota, chhalni and katoris and let them dry.
  3. Prepare roli, rice, sweets and flowers in advance.
  4. Arrange everything on the thali to check that it fits without crowding.
  5. Keep the diya, oil or ghee and a lighter ready in a safe place.

A complete set or separate pieces

A matching set looks coordinated and saves time. Buying separately lets you reuse items you already have. If you want everything in one finish, choose a set that includes the thali, kalash or lota, chhalni, diya and katoris.

After the festival

Wipe each item gently, dry it and store it wrapped in soft cloth. Decorated items can be reused next year or for other pujas.

Frequently asked questions

What items are needed for a Karwa Chauth thali?

Most families use a diya, a karwa or lota, a chhalni, katoris for roli, rice and sweets, and basic puja items. Follow your own family custom for the full list.

Can I reuse a decorated Karwa Chauth thali?

Yes. With gentle cleaning and careful storage, it can be reused every year and for other pujas.

How early should I get the items ready?

Order or prepare at least one to two weeks before, and do the final check the day before the festival.
