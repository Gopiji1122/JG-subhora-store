How to Choose a Decorative Pooja Thali for a Wedding

A pooja thali is used again and again during a wedding. It appears when guests are welcomed, during aarti, for tilak and for ritual offerings. Because it is seen by everyone, it should be beautiful, and because it is handled often, it should also be sturdy. This guide explains how to choose one with confidence.

Quick answerChoose a 12-inch stainless steel thali with firmly fixed decoration, confirm exactly what is included, and order several weeks before the wedding so there is time for dispatch.

Where a thali is used at a wedding

  • Welcoming the groom, the bride or the guests with aarti and tilak
  • Holding the diya, roli, rice and flowers during rituals
  • Carrying offerings and sweets
  • Presenting gifts or items for exchange between families

Many families keep more than one thali ready, so that one can be used while another is being prepared. Decide how many you need before you order.

1Check the size

A 12-inch thali is a comfortable size for aarti. It holds a diya, small katoris and flowers without feeling crowded, and it is still light enough to carry. Smaller plates work for single offerings but can look cramped when you add several items.

2Look at the base material

Stainless steel is a practical base for a thali used at events. It is strong, does not rust easily and is simple to wipe clean. Before ordering, ask the seller what the base is made of and what the decoration is made from.

3Ask how the decoration is attached

Mirror pieces, stones and pearls should be firmly fixed. Ask what adhesive was used. Water-resistant adhesive helps when the thali is wiped or gently rinsed, but a decorated thali should never be scrubbed or soaked for long periods.

4Choose a style that suits the occasion

  • Red with mirror work: Traditional and festive, a classic choice for wedding rituals.
  • Gold and pearl details: Elegant and works well with rich wedding colours.
  • Coloured stones: Adds a lively, celebratory look that stands out in photographs.

5Confirm what is included

Photos often show a full set, but the listing may sell only the thali. Always confirm whether katoris, a kalash and a diya are included. A complete set is convenient if you want everything to match, while a single thali is enough if you already have the other items.

6Think about a matching kalash

A decorated kalash is used in many wedding rituals. Choosing a kalash with a similar finish to your thali makes the ritual table look coordinated. Our Decorated Kalash for Wedding has a red mirror-work finish with pearl details and colourful stones.

7Order early

Decorated items are prepared with care, and shipping takes time. Order several weeks before the wedding, if possible, and ask for current photos on WhatsApp so you can check the colour and finish. Small differences can occur in handmade work and in how screens show colour.

Looking after the thali after the wedding

Wipe it gently with a soft cloth, dry it, and store it wrapped in cloth or in a box. A well-kept decorated thali can be reused at festivals like Diwali and Karwa Chauth, or kept as a memory of the wedding.

Frequently asked questions

How many thalis do I need for a wedding?

It depends on the rituals and the number of families involved. Many people keep two or three ready so one is always available.

Can I use a decorated thali for aarti with a diya?

Yes, but place the diya on a stable base and keep the flame away from decorations. Never leave a lit diya unattended.
