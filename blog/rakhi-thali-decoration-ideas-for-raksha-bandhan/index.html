Rakhi Thali Decoration Ideas for Raksha Bandhan

On Raksha Bandhan, the rakhi thali carries the rakhi, roli, rice and sweets, so it is worth making it look special. This guide shares rakhi thali decoration ideas, a simple way to arrange the items, and tips for choosing a thali you can reuse for other festivals.

  • ⏱ 3 min read
  • 📅 Updated 23 Oct 2026
  • ✍ JG Subhora Store
Quick answerUse a clean, decorated thali in red and gold, keep the rakhi, roli, rice, diya and sweets neatly arranged, and choose a thali that can also be used for Diwali and Bhai Dooj.

What goes on a rakhi thali

  • The rakhi
  • Roli or kumkum for the tilak
  • Rice (akshat)
  • A diya, if your family lights one
  • Sweets in a small katori
  • Any other items your family uses

Customs differ between families, so follow your own tradition for the exact items.

Rakhi thali decoration ideas

  • Red and gold: A classic festive combination.
  • Mirror and pearl work: Adds sparkle and looks lovely in photographs.
  • Swastik or flower centre: A clear central motif makes the thali look balanced.
  • Ribbons and flowers: A small ribbon or a few petals add a soft, personal touch.
  • Colourful rakhis as decoration: Place a few rakhis on the edge as part of the display.

How to arrange the thali

  1. Wipe the thali clean and dry it.
  2. Place the diya at the front or centre, where it is stable.
  3. Set the rakhi in the middle or on one side.
  4. Put roli, rice and sweets in katoris around the edge.
  5. Add a few flower petals last for colour.

A simple do-it-yourself idea

If you want to decorate a plain thali at home, start with a clean steel plate and fix a flower or swastik motif at the centre. Add a ring of pearls or small stones around it, then finish the rim with a simple border. Let the adhesive dry fully before placing roli, rice and sweets on the thali, and keep the design light so the plate stays easy to hold.

Choosing a rakhi thali

Pick a thali that is a comfortable size, easy to hold and neat in finish. A 12-inch thali holds the rakhi, katoris and diya without crowding. Ask what the base is made of and how the decoration is fixed.

Reuse it for other festivals

A good decorated thali does not need to be put away after one day. It also works for Bhai Dooj, Diwali puja and other family functions, which gives you much better value than a single-use plate.

Gifting a thali

A decorated thali also makes a thoughtful gift, for a brother, a sister or a newly married couple. Add a small set of katoris to make the gift feel complete.

Frequently asked questions

Which colour is best for a rakhi thali?

Red and gold is the traditional favourite, but any clean, bright colour combination works. Choose one that matches your family custom and home.

Can I use a pooja thali as a rakhi thali?

Yes. A decorated pooja thali works well for Raksha Bandhan, Bhai Dooj, Diwali and other festivals.

How early should I order a rakhi thali?

Order or prepare at least one to two weeks before the festival so there is time for dispatch and delivery.
