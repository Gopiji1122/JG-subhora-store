About JG Subhora Store

JG Subhora Store is an online manufacturer and seller of decorative puja essentials, serving retail and wholesale customers from Rajasthan, India.

Our catalogue focuses on decorative thalis and plates, katoris, small kalash and lota, big kalash, chhalni, and complete puja sets. Availability and the exact contents of a set are confirmed before an order is accepted.

Made with care

Our products are prepared with attention to neat finishing and presentation. Decorative stones are applied by hand using water-resistant adhesive. Each item is checked before dispatch, although handmade work can have small variations.

Product photos and descriptions

We aim to show actual products in our photos. Because of lighting, screen settings and handmade finishing, the delivered item may have minor differences. Please ask for current photos and confirm specifications if a particular detail matters to you.

Ordering and delivery

Customers can enquire and place orders through WhatsApp. We confirm current prices, availability, shipping charges and estimated dispatch timing before payment. We do not offer COD. Orders are generally shipped through India Post Speed Post across India, subject to service availability.

Care and support

Decorative products should be handled gently and kept away from prolonged soaking, harsh chemicals and rough scrubbing. Water-resistant adhesive does not mean that the whole product is waterproof in every condition.
