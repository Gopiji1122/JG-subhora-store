About Us

At JG Subhora Store, we believe every puja accessory should reflect care, beauty, and attention to detail. Based in Rajasthan, India, we manufacture decorative puja items and complete puja sets for individual customers, families, retailers, and bulk buyers.

Our Story

Our goal is to create premium-quality decorative products with distinctive designs and carefully finished details. Every design has its own character, and we work to make each piece attractive, neat, and thoughtfully presented.

Our Products

Our collection includes decorative thalis and plates, katoris, small kalash and lota, big kalash, chhalni, and complete puja sets. Customers can explore individual accessories or choose a set with the items specified on its product page.

Careful Craftsmanship

We pay attention to cleanliness, decorative placement, and finishing throughout the making process. Where a design includes decorative stones, each stone is applied by hand using a high-quality water-resistant adhesive selected for the work. We aim to use quality materials suited to each product and to maintain care across our collection.

A Clear and Convenient Shopping Experience

We provide photographs of the actual items we offer so customers can review the design before ordering. Customers can contact us on WhatsApp to ask questions, confirm product details, and place an order. We ship across India, subject to applicable shipping terms, and take care when packaging products for transit.

Returns and exchanges are handled according to our published Shipping & Returns Policy. Please review product details, included items, and applicable terms before placing an order.

Our Commitment

We are continually working to improve our designs, finishing, and customer experience. Whether you are choosing one decorative accessory or a complete puja set, we want to make it easier to find products that suit your needs.

Thank you for visiting JG Subhora Store and considering our products for your special occasions.
